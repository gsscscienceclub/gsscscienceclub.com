About Us

Welcome to GSSC Science Club


Govt. Shaheed Suhrawardy College Science Club was established in 2019 to foster a scientific mindset among the students. Under the direct guidance of our Chief Patron, Principal Prof. Dr. Kakali Mukherjee, we aim to create a dynamic platform for young innovators.


Our journey is filled with science fairs, Olympiads, research workshops, and wall magazine exhibitions. Join us to explore the limitless possibilities of science!

Core Objectives

Scientific Curiosity

To encourage students to question the world around them and develop a keen sense of scientific curiosity.

Innovation & Projects

Providing hands-on experience and support to students for creating innovative science projects.

Olympiads & Fairs

Preparing and organizing students to participate in national and international science fairs and Olympiads.
